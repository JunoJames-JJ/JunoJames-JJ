### A little about me

Hi! I'm Juno, a software engineer who's touched pretty much every layer of the stack: native iOS apps, ERP and warehouse management systems, CRM platforms, full-stack web apps, backend APIs, you name it. Somewhere along the way I fell down the AI rabbit hole, and now I split my time between solid engineering and figuring out how to make machines a little smarter.

- 🎓 Just wrapped up a Postgraduate Certificate in AI & Machine Learning, graduated on the **President's Honour Roll**, which I'm still a little proud of 🏆
- 🛠️ These days I'm building LLM-powered apps, playing with prompts, and obsessing over whether my models actually work (evaluation nerd, guilty as charged)
- 🏢 Before that: enterprise systems (ERP, CRM, warehouse management), full-stack web apps, and native iOS, real software for real businesses
- 🔭 Currently poking around agentic workflows and RAG
- 💬 Happy to talk about full-stack development, enterprise systems, LLM apps, or why your model's accuracy number might be lying to you
- ☕ Powered by curiosity and probably too much coffee
